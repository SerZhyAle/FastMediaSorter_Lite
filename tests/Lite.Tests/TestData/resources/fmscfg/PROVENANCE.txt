FMSCFG interchange fixtures - vendored artifacts, not a vendored contract
========================================================================

canonical-v1.fmscfg, canonical-v2.fmscfg
  The two canonical vectors of the FMSCFG contract (catalog: config-interchange/CONFIG_FORMAT.md,
  document version 2.1, read 2026-10-02). Extracted VERBATIM from the contract's own code fences by
  script, byte for byte, no trailing newline. They are the shared bytes the parser is pinned to; they
  must never be regenerated from this repository's serializer.

android-export-synthetic.fmscfg, companion-export-synthetic.fmscfg
  SYNTHETIC. Written by hand to the SHAPE the Android response 101 section 11 describes (one root, one
  lan path, schemaVersion 2, readOnly omitted, slideshowInterval written, password and fingerprint empty)
  and the Share Manager's own producer (readOnly written on every root, a destination with readOnly:true
  and isDestination:true, an ipv6 path, an accessNote). Dummy credentials and documentation addresses
  (RFC 5737 / 3849) only. They are not captures from a named build; a live Android export remains a manual
  acceptance item.
