## What this repository must do to stay conformant

- A change to the bars or the halo that changes what the user sees - a constant, the easing, the fade
  curve, the durations, the seam - is an **amendment in the catalog first** (a version bump and a log
  row, vectors regenerated by the reference script), then the code. This product owns the contracts, so
  it writes the amendment itself; nobody else's code moves with it.
- The math lives in one tested place citing the rules and runs the catalog vectors - **not yet**: the
  constants are still private members of `Main_Form` and no test runs the vectors (rung 1 of the
  contract's section 5 is not held; `specification 056` (local specification) is the open work, the
  registry exception runs to 2026-12-31). What does hold today, by reading on 2026-10-07: the numbers the
  rules name agree with the files (the 15 ms frame, the 350 ms slow duration halved for medium and
  quartered for fast, gamma 1.4 with 12 stops, the quadratic ease-out, the 2 px overlap and seam, the
  100-entry step, the 7 % trim, the 4 % deviation, the 0.0006 smoothing factor, the 50 ms redraw
  throttle), and the stored speed reads `medium` when missing, empty or unknown
  (`HaloAnimationSpeedTests`, the only rule under a test).
- The reduced-motion answer of `LETTERBOX-HALO` rule 9 is the same `ReduceMotionActive()` the audio
  backdrop uses: the system's "Animation effects" setting wins, the product's switch may only add
  stillness.
